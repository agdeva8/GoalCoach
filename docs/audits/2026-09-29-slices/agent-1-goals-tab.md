## Summary

The Goals tab and its three modal flows (AddGoalDialog, ActionPromptModal, ChatModal) render the right shapes but have **two clear functional bugs** and a **systemic a11y/touch-target deficit** on mobile. The functional bugs: (1) confirming a goal in `AddGoalDialog` closes the modal but never re-fetches parent state, so the user is told they have no goals even after a successful confirm (CRITICAL — works only after reload); (2) any path that drops the guest session (e.g. clearing localStorage) leaves the Goals tab silently broken — empty panel, no error UI, no sign-in CTA — because `/api/state` returns 401 and there's no error/inline-signin component wired up (CRITICAL). On mobile (390×844), every goal action button, every modal close X, every modal footer button, the chat Send button, and the Guest sign-in CTA measure **well below the 44 pt Apple/Google touch target minimum** — the worst being `X className="w-4 h-4"` at 16×16. Focus management is partially broken: `ActionPromptModal` claims `autoFocus` on its textarea but `CenteredDialog`'s `setTimeout(...,0)` refocuses the wrapper div, and `AddGoalDialog`'s tile→chat step transition never moves focus into the textarea. Visually the Welcome toast and the FAB collide in the bottom-right (desktop) / bottom-center (mobile) corner, the loading state is a bare "loading…" line with no skeleton, and the first-visit `AddGoalDialog` auto-opens without opt-out. Polish items: the focused-task dialog drops the goal name from its title, `Enter` sends in the action prompt on mobile with no Shift+Enter escape hatch, the category grid leaves an empty trailing cell, and the tablist overflows horizontally on 390 px without a scroll affordance. No regression in modal stacking order (z-60 modal > z-40 FAB ✓); the latent Sonner-toaster z-index risk should be confirmed with a real 401 toast in a closed-modal context.
